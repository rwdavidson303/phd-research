## UNITED STATES

### Tier 1 -- Best Fit (AACSB + No GRE/GMAT + Online/Hybrid)

#### 1. Texas State University (McCoy College of Business) -- DBA
- **Accreditation:** AACSB
- **Format:** 100% fully online, asynchronous. No residency.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** $36,900 total (flat-rate, 5 payments/year)
- **Duration:** 3 years (cohort-based)
- **Notes:** Launched Fall 2025. Lowest-cost AACSB program found. Dissertation is self-directed.
- **URL:** https://www.mccoy.txst.edu/graduate/dba.html
- **Rating: EXCELLENT**

#### 2. Marshall University (Brad D. Smith Schools of Business) -- DBA
- **Accreditation:** AACSB
- **Format:** Hybrid. 5 on-campus weekend residencies/year (Fri-Sat) Years 1-2, otherwise online.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** ~$850/credit hour (~$51K estimated total)
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Notes:** WRDS/Compustat/CRSP database access included. Member of Executive DBA Council.
- **URL:** https://www.marshall.edu/cob/graduate/doctor-of-business-administration/
- **Rating: STRONG**

#### 3. Drexel University (LeBow College of Business) -- Executive DBA
- **Accreditation:** AACSB (STEM-designated)
- **Format:** Blended. Two face-to-face residencies per 10-week quarter (Thu-Sat, Philadelphia).
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** ~$128,000 total
- **Duration:** 2.5 years (accelerated)
- **Notes:** R1 research institution. Supply chain faculty present.
- **URL:** https://www.lebow.drexel.edu/academics/doctorate/executive-dba
- **Rating: STRONG** (frequent residencies and high cost)

#### 4. University of South Florida (Muma College of Business) -- DBA

## EUROPE

### Tier 1 -- Best Fit (Triple Crown + No GMAT + Online/Hybrid)

#### 1. Aston Business School -- Executive DBA (Online)
- **Country:** UK
- **Accreditation:** Triple Crown (AACSB + EQUIS + AMBA)
- **Format:** Fully online, no compulsory live lectures, no fixed schedule. Study anytime, anywhere.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** GBP 39,000 total (~$50K); monthly payment options
- **Duration:** 4-6 years
- **Notes:** Perhaps the strongest match on ALL criteria globally. Triple-crown, fully online, affordable.
- **URL:** https://online.aston.ac.uk/online-courses/business/executive-doctor-of-business-administration-dba
- **Rating: EXCELLENT**

#### 2. Edinburgh Business School / Heriot-Watt University -- DBA
- **Country:** UK (Scotland) -- globally accessible online
- **Accreditation:** Triple Crown (AACSB + EQUIS + AMBA)
- **Format:** Fully online, self-study distance learning. No residencies. No fixed start date.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** Per-module pricing; estimated GBP 15,000-25,000 total (~$19K-32K)
- **Duration:** ~5 years average
- **Notes:** 25+ years of online delivery experience. Most flexible option in Europe. Also has Dubai campus.
- **URL:** https://www.hw.ac.uk/ebs/study/dba
- **Rating: EXCELLENT**

#### 3. University of Liverpool Management School -- Executive DBA
- **Country:** UK
- **Accreditation:** Triple Crown (AACSB + EQUIS + AMBA)
- **Format:** Hybrid -- primarily online with 4 weekend on-campus residencies during first 2 years. Thesis phase fully distance.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** See course page; 15% discount available; alumni receive GBP 2,000/yr discount
- **Duration:** 4.5 years standard; up to 7.5 years
- **Notes:** Action-research model fits applied procurement dissertation work well.
- **URL:** https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/courses/executive-doctorate-in-business-administration
- **Rating: STRONG**

#### 4. Grenoble Ecole de Management (GEM) -- Global DBA
- **Country:** France (Paris + Grenoble + global hubs)
- **Accreditation:** Triple Crown (AACSB + EQUIS + AMBA)
- **Format:** Blended -- online workshops + ~3 in-person residential seminars/year.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** Merit scholarships up to EUR 6,000; contact dba@grenoble-em.com
- **Duration:** 3 years
- **Notes:** First DBA program in Europe. Supply chain and sustainability are active research areas.
- **URL:** https://www.grenoble-em.com/en/programs/doctorate-of-business-administration-dba/
- **Rating: STRONG**

#### 5. Nottingham Business School (Nottingham Trent University) -- DBA
- **Country:** UK
- **Accreditation:** AACSB + EQUIS (double accreditation)
- **Format:** Blended -- distance/online with some in-person components.
- **GRE/GMAT:** NOT REQUIRED
- **Tuition:** Contact school; generally more affordable than Russell Group
- **Duration:** 4-6 years
- **Notes:** One of the oldest DBA programs in the UK (20+ years, 100+ completions).
- **URL:** https://www.dba-compass.com/programs/nottingham-trent-university-nottingham-business-school/
