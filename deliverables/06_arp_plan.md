## 1. Overview: The ARP-Dissertation Pipeline

The Kelley DBA program includes a four-course Applied Research Practicum (ARP I-IV) that produces a publishable research paper by the end of Year 2. The dissertation is a separate, larger research project conducted in Year 3.

For this research program, the ARP and dissertation are designed as a **pipeline**: the ARP produces a pilot study (2 cases) that directly feeds into the full dissertation (5 cases). Nothing is wasted. Every piece of ARP work becomes a building block for the dissertation.

## 2. ARP I: Systematic Literature Review (Summer 2027, BUS 6500)

### Purpose
Conduct a rigorous systematic review of qualitative and mixed-methods research on source selection decision-making in public procurement. This review establishes the empirical and theoretical foundation for the case study and identifies the specific gaps the dissertation will address.

### Scope and Search Strategy

**Research question for the review:** What does existing qualitative evidence reveal about how procurement professionals make source selection method decisions, and what organizational and institutional factors shape the outcomes of those decisions?

**Databases:** Web of Science, Scopus, ProQuest Dissertations & Theses, JSTOR, Google Scholar, Defense Technical Information Center (DTIC)

**Search terms:** Combinations of: source selection, best value, LPTA, lowest price, tradeoff, procurement decision-making, contracting officer, acquisition workforce, qualitative, case study, interview

**Inclusion criteria:**
- Published 2000-2027
- English language
- Empirical studies (qualitative, quantitative, or mixed) examining source selection method choice, procurement decision-making, or contracting officer behavior
- Government procurement context (federal, state, local, or international)

**Exclusion criteria:**
- Pure policy commentary without empirical data
- Private-sector procurement studies without public-sector comparison
- Studies focused solely on commodity purchasing (not services/IT)

### Expected Output
- 25-35 page literature review paper
- Systematic review table: study, method, context, findings, gaps
- Identification of theoretical frameworks used in existing research
- Explicit map of gaps that the dissertation addresses
- Updated reference list incorporating 2025-2027 publications

### How It Feeds the Dissertation
This paper becomes the foundation for Chapter 2 (Literature Review) of the dissertation. It will be substantially revised and extended during the dissertation phase to incorporate the study's own findings and theoretical refinements.

## 3. ARP II: Research Protocol and IRB (Fall 2027, BUS 6501)

### Purpose
Develop the complete case study protocol, obtain IRB approval, and begin participant recruitment. By the end of ARP II, the study should be ready to begin data collection.

### Key Deliverables

**3.1 Case Study Protocol**
Following Yin (2018), the protocol will include:
- Study overview and research questions
- Data collection procedures for each case
- Interview protocol (fully piloted and refined)
- Document analysis checklist
- Archival data extraction plan (agency-level data from the quant dataset)
- Coding architecture (6 code groups, 43+ codes with definitions)
- Cross-case analysis plan
- Quality assurance procedures

**3.2 IRB Application**
- Draft full IRB application for IU's IRB
- Develop informed consent forms (separate versions for government and vendor participants)
- Address federal employee interview considerations
- Develop data security plan (encrypted storage, anonymization procedures)
- Target: IRB approval by end of Fall 2027 quarter

**3.3 Interview Guide Pilot Testing**
- Conduct 2-3 practice interviews with procurement professionals (not from target agencies)
- Revise interview questions based on pilot feedback
- Test recording equipment and transcription workflow
- Calibrate interview length (target 60-90 minutes)

**3.4 Participant Recruitment**
- Send initial recruitment emails to professional contacts at target agencies
- Submit formal research access requests to agency public affairs/academic liaison offices
- Engage NCMA chapter contacts for snowball referrals
- Begin scheduling Case 1 and Case 2 interviews for Winter 2028

### How It Feeds the Dissertation
The protocol document becomes Chapter 3 (Methodology) of the dissertation. The IRB approval carries forward to the dissertation phase without requiring a new application (assuming no protocol changes). Participant relationships established during recruitment carry into Year 3 data collection.

## 4. ARP III: Pilot Data Collection (Winter 2028, BUS 6502)

### Purpose
Conduct the first two cases of the five-case study. These pilot cases serve three functions: (1) test and refine the research protocol, (2) generate substantive findings, and (3) provide a demonstrated track record of execution for the dissertation proposal defense.

### Recommended Pilot Cases

**Case 1: GSA Federal Acquisition Service**
- *Rationale for piloting first:* LPTA-heavy, efficiency-driven culture; the government's "marketplace" agency. Tests the Kelman process quality thesis (can a well-run LPTA process produce strong outcomes?). Civilian agency with relatively open culture for academic research.
- *Participants:* 4-5 government COs + 2-3 GSA Schedule/OASIS+ vendors
- *Data collection period:* January-February 2028

**Case 2: Air Force or Navy Acquisition Office**
- *Rationale:* Tradeoff-heavy post-NDAA Section 813; high complexity services. Provides maximum contrast with Case 1 (different method preference, different culture, different complexity level).
- *Participants:* 4-5 government COs + 2-3 defense contractor program managers
- *Data collection period:* February-March 2028

### Analysis Activities
- Transcribe all interviews (use professional transcription service)
- Import transcripts into QDAS project
- Conduct First Cycle coding (initial/open) for both cases
- Conduct Second Cycle coding (focused) for both cases
- Write within-case narratives (30-50 pages each)
- Write analytic memos comparing the two cases
- Refine coding architecture based on what emerges from the data
- Identify protocol adjustments needed for remaining cases

### Expected Output
- 14-16 coded interview transcripts
- 2 within-case narratives
- Refined coding architecture (with emergent codes added)
- Preliminary cross-case comparison notes
- Protocol refinements for Cases 3-5

### How It Feeds the Dissertation
Cases 1-2 become the first two cases in the dissertation's Chapter 4 (Results). The within-case narratives are revised and incorporated directly. The coding architecture is proven and refined. The protocol is validated. At the dissertation proposal defense (Fall 2028), you present real data from two completed cases -- not just a plan.

## 5. ARP IV: Research Paper (Spring 2028, BUS 6503)

### Purpose
Write and submit the ARP research paper: a dual-case qualitative study of source selection decision-making. This paper stands alone as a publishable contribution and also serves as the empirical core from which the dissertation expands.

### Paper Structure

**Working Title:** "Method, Context, or Workforce? A Dual-Case Study of Source Selection Decision-Making in Federal Procurement"

**Target length:** 8,000-10,000 words (journal article format)

**Outline:**
1. Introduction: the policy puzzle and the quantitative evidence that method alone doesn't predict outcomes
2. Literature review: TCE, institutional theory, Kelman (condensed from ARP I)
3. Methods: dual-case study design, participant descriptions, coding approach
4. Case 1 findings (GSA)
5. Case 2 findings (Air Force/Navy)
6. Cross-case analysis: convergent and divergent patterns
7. Discussion: what the two cases reveal about when method matters
8. Implications: for theory, practice, and the planned 5-case expansion
9. Limitations and future research (previewing the full dissertation)

### Target Journals for the ARP Paper

| Priority | Journal | Rationale |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1st | Journal of Public Procurement | Natural home; directly on topic; faster review |
| 2nd | Defense Acquisition Research Journal | Practitioner audience; DoD case relevance |
| 3rd | Public Performance and Management Review | Performance measurement angle |

### How It Feeds the Dissertation
The ARP paper is a self-contained contribution that also establishes the empirical and analytical foundation for the full five-case dissertation. The dissertation extends the ARP by:
- Adding 3 more cases (Cases 3-5)
- Conducting full cross-case analysis across all 5 agencies
- Integrating the quantitative pilot data systematically
- Developing more robust theoretical propositions
- Expanding the vendor perspective analysis

## 6. Comprehensive Exams (Summer 2028)

### Preparation Strategy

The comprehensive exams cover research methodology, statistics, and seminar coursework. Preparation should integrate the dissertation research with the broader curriculum:

**Methodology component:**
- Articulate the sequential explanatory design rationale
- Defend the case study method choice against alternatives
- Explain the relationship between the quantitative pilot and the qualitative study
- Discuss trustworthiness criteria and how the study addresses each

**Statistics component:**
- Explain the quantitative pilot study design and findings
- Discuss propensity score matching, OLS, GLM, logistic regression
- Address limitations of quasi-experimental designs and the value of mixed methods
- Connect statistical findings to the qualitative research questions

**Seminar component:**
- Integrate leadership theory (MGMT 6300) with procurement decision-making
- Connect organizational ethics (MGMT 6301) to procurement integrity
- Apply cross-disciplinary decision-making frameworks (BUS 6300) to source selection

### Key Reading List for Exams

- Edmondson & McManus (2007): Methodological fit
