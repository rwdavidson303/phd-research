## Top 25 Most Influential People in US Government Procurement

This section profiles the leading academics, practitioners, and policymakers who have shaped government RFP processes and procurement policy in the United States.

### Categories

- **Academics & Researchers** - Scholars who study procurement economics, public value, and contracting
- **Government Officials** - Current and former leaders at OFPP, GAO, GSA, and DoD acquisition
- **Legal Scholars** - Experts in government contracting law and bid protests
- **Policy Advocates** - Leaders at NIGP, NASPO, ABA Public Contract Law Section
- **International Experts** - OECD, World Bank, and other international procurement specialists
