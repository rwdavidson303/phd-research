**AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process):** A structured multi-criteria decision analysis method used to evaluate procurement proposals by decomposing the evaluation into a hierarchy of criteria and sub-criteria, assigning weights, and scoring proposals against each criterion. Used extensively in Singapore's GeBIZ system.

**Beneficial Ownership:** The identification of the natural person(s) who ultimately own or control a legal entity. Beneficial ownership disclosure in procurement prevents the use of shell companies to conceal conflicts of interest, sanctions evasion, or corrupt relationships. Chile achieved 59 percent compliance within three months of implementation.

**Best-in-Class (BIC):** A US federal designation for government-wide contract vehicles that meet rigorous criteria for pricing, terms, and management. BIC spending exceeded $120 billion in FY2023.

**Best-Value Tradeoff:** A US federal procurement evaluation methodology in which the government evaluates proposals based on both price and non-price factors (technical merit, past performance, management approach) and may award to other than the lowest-priced offeror if the additional value justifies the additional cost.

**Category Management:** A strategic approach to procurement that organizes spending into logical categories and leverages the government's collective buying power to achieve better pricing, terms, and outcomes.

**ComprasNet/PNCP:** Brazil's electronic procurement platform. ComprasNet is being migrated to the Portal Nacional de Contratacoes Publicas (PNCP) under the 2021 procurement law.

**CPARS (Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System):** The US federal system for recording contractor performance evaluations. Data is restricted and not publicly accessible, limiting research and transparency.

**Crown Commercial Service (CCS):** The UK government's central purchasing body, managing GBP 30.3 billion in commercial agreements and delivering GBP 4.9 billion in annual savings.

**DFARS (Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement):** The Department of Defense supplement to the FAR, adding defense-specific procurement rules.

**FAR (Federal Acquisition Regulation):** The primary body of rules governing US federal procurement. Exceeds 2,000 pages and is supplemented by dozens of agency-specific regulations.

**GeBIZ (Government Electronic Business):** Singapore's centralized e-procurement portal serving 120+ government agencies.

**Ge-GP (Electronic Government Procurement):** Georgia's e-procurement system, built in 10 months for under $1 million, credited with reducing corruption from 97 percent to 3 percent.

**Green Public Procurement (GPP):** The EU framework for incorporating environmental criteria into procurement evaluation and contract requirements.

**HUBZone:** A US SBA program providing contracting preferences to businesses in Historically Underutilized Business Zones.

**Indigenous Procurement Policy (IPP):** Australia's policy mandating government procurement with indigenous-owned businesses, generating $12.9 billion in cumulative opportunities and 83,500+ contracts.

**Innovation Partnership:** An EU procurement procedure type that combines R&D and subsequent commercial procurement in a single process, addressing the "valley of death" between development and deployment.

**KONEPS (Korea ON-line E-Procurement System):** South Korea's single-window e-procurement platform connecting 68,000+ entities with 430,000+ suppliers, saving an estimated $8 billion annually.

**Life-Cycle Costing (LCC):** An evaluation methodology that considers total cost over the useful life of a product or service, including acquisition, operation, maintenance, and disposal costs. Formalized in EU procurement directives.

**LPTA (Lowest Price Technically Acceptable):** A US federal procurement evaluation methodology in which the contract is awarded to the lowest-priced offeror that meets minimum technical requirements. No credit for exceeding requirements.

**MAT (Most Advantageous Tender):** The evaluation standard established by the UK Procurement Act 2023, replacing the EU's MEAT. Considers the full range of public value—social, environmental, and economic.

**MEAT (Most Economically Advantageous Tender):** The EU standard evaluation criterion requiring consideration of best price-quality ratio, not just lowest price.

**Mercado Publico:** Chile's electronic marketplace for all public procurement, operated by ChileCompra.

**OCDS (Open Contracting Data Standard):** An international standard for publishing structured, machine-readable procurement data. Adopted by Chile and 50+ other jurisdictions.

**Once-Only Principle:** The principle—pioneered by Estonia—that citizens and businesses should provide any piece of information to the government only once, with that information shared across all entities that need it.

**OPO (Office of the Procurement Ombudsman):** Canada's independent body for reviewing procurement complaints, mediating disputes, and recommending practice improvements.

**OTA (Other Transaction Authority):** A US procurement mechanism that allows federal agencies to enter into agreements outside the FAR for prototype development and follow-on production.

**PCP (Pre-Commercial Procurement):** A procurement approach that funds multiple vendors through progressive R&D phases (concept, prototype, pilot) before commercial procurement. Pioneered by Denmark and promoted by the EU.

**Pregao:** Brazil's electronic reverse auction procurement method, in which vendors compete by lowering prices in real time and qualification is verified after the price competition.

**Proportionality Principle:** The principle—codified in New Zealand's procurement rules—that the level of procurement process should be proportional to the value and risk of the procurement.

**SAM.gov (System for Award Management):** The primary US federal platform for vendor registration, opportunity search, and entity management.

**SBIR (Small Business Innovation Research):** A US program providing competitive awards to small businesses for R&D with commercial potential. Managed across federal agencies.

**Section 809 Panel:** A congressionally mandated advisory panel (2016-2019) that produced over 1,000 recommendations for streamlining US defense acquisition regulations.

**Social Value:** The broader economic, social, and environmental benefits generated through procurement, beyond the direct goods and services purchased. Mandatory evaluation criterion in UK central government contracts (minimum 10 percent weighting).

**TED (Tenders Electronic Daily):** The EU's online platform for publishing public procurement notices above threshold values.

**USProcure:** The proposed unified US federal procurement platform combining vendor registration, opportunity publication, bid submission, contract management, and analytics.

**Value for Money (VfM):** The optimal combination of whole-of-life costs and quality to meet the user's requirement. Core principle of Australian, New Zealand, and UK procurement frameworks.

**X-Road:** Estonia's distributed data exchange platform enabling secure, real-time data sharing between 929 institutions. Foundation of Estonia's digital government ecosystem and exported to 20+ countries.
