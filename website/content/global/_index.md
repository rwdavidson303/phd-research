## Successful Procurement Systems Worldwide

This section documents public procurement systems around the world that operate with high satisfaction, transparency, and value-for-money outcomes. These case studies provide international context for the dissertation's focus on US procurement reform.

### Countries & Systems Covered

- **South Korea** - KONEPS (Korea ON-line E-Procurement System)
- **Chile** - ChileCompra / Mercado Publico
- **United Kingdom** - Crown Commercial Service & Social Value Act
- **Australia** - Commonwealth Procurement Rules
- **Canada** - Office of the Procurement Ombudsman
- **New Zealand** - Government Procurement Rules & Broader Outcomes
- **European Union** - Public Procurement Directives & MEAT Criteria
- **Singapore** - GeBIZ Procurement System
- **Estonia** - e-Procurement & Digital Government
- **Georgia** - Electronic Procurement Reform
- **Denmark/Nordic Countries** - Innovation Partnerships
- **Brazil** - Pregao Electronic Reverse Auctions

### Key Themes Across Successful Systems

1. **Digital-first platforms** that increase transparency and reduce corruption
2. **Value-based evaluation** beyond lowest price (MEAT, best-value frameworks)
3. **Simplified processes** that reduce administrative burden while maintaining accountability
4. **Open data** that enables oversight and research
5. **Feedback loops** incorporating user satisfaction and performance measurement
6. **Proportionality** matching process rigor to procurement risk and value
